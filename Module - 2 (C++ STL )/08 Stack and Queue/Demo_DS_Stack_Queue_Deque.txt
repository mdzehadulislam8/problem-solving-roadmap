==================================================
                STACK, QUEUE & DEQUE
==================================================


1. STACK (LIFO – Last In, First Out)
----------------------------------

A Stack is a data structure where
the element that is inserted last is removed first.

Real-life example:
A stack of books or plates

- Books are placed from the top
- Books are removed from the top


Example & Result:

Push order:
10 -> 20 -> 30

Stack representation:

Top
 |
 | 30
 | 20
 | 10

Pop operation:
Pop -> 30

Result:
30 is removed first because it was inserted last.

That is why it follows:
Last In -> First Out

Uses:
- Function calls (Call Stack)
- Undo / Redo operations
- Expression evaluation


--------------------------------------------------


2. QUEUE (FIFO – First In, First Out)
------------------------------------

A Queue is a data structure where
the element that is inserted first is removed first.

Real-life example:
People standing in a line

- The person who comes first is served first


Example & Result:

Enqueue order:
10 -> 20 -> 30

Queue representation:

Front -> 10 | 20 | 30 <- Rear

Dequeue operation:
Dequeue -> 10

Result:
10 is removed first because it was inserted first.

That is why it follows:
First In -> First Out

Uses:
- CPU scheduling
- Printer queue
- Breadth First Search (BFS)


--------------------------------------------------


3. DEQUE (Double Ended Queue)
----------------------------

A Deque is an advanced form of queue where
elements can be inserted and removed from both
the front and the rear.

Real-life example:
Train coaches

- Coaches can be added from the front or the back
- Coaches can be removed from the front or the back


Example & Result:

Operations:
InsertFront(20)
InsertBack(10)
InsertBack(30)

Deque representation:

Front -> 20 | 10 | 30 <- Rear

Delete operations:
DeleteFront -> 20
DeleteBack  -> 30

Result:
Remaining element = 10

That is why it is called:
Insertion and deletion from both ends

Uses:
- Sliding Window problems
- Palindrome checking
- Implementing Stack and Queue
